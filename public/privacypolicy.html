Infinite Options Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Bond Trivia (the "App"), published by Infinite Options, LLC ("Infinite Options," "we," "us," or "our"). We respect your privacy, and the App is designed to work without collecting your personal information.

Summary

  • The App does not require an account.
  • The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information.
  • Your quiz answers, scores, and progress are not saved or sent anywhere. They exist only while you are playing.
  • The App does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.

Information We Collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifier, usage data, or any other personal information through the App. Because we do not collect or retain data, there is nothing for us to sell, share, or disclose to third parties.

When the App downloads content from our servers, our hosting provider may process standard technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver that content. We do not use this information to identify you, and we do not store it or link it to you.

Tracking and Advertising

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, we do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not show third-party advertising in the App.

Information the App Stores May Collect

You download the App from the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google may collect information about your downloads, purchases, and device under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your personal information from them. We may see aggregated, non-identifying statistics such as the number of downloads or crash summaries.

Contacting Us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use them only to respond to you and keep them only for up to 12 months. We do not share them with third parties or use them for marketing. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Your Rights

Because the App does not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete about your use of it. If you have emailed us and want your messages deleted, or if you have any privacy question, contact us using the details below, and we will respond promptly.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Because the App does not collect or store personal information, there is no personal data on our systems for the App to put at risk. Any email you send us is protected using reasonable safeguards.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we change how the App handles information, we will post the updated policy at https://infiniteoptions.com/privacypolicy.html and update the "Last updated" date before the change takes effect.
